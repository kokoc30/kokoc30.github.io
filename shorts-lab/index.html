Shorts Lab - Psychology Neon

A private creator automation tool for the Psychology Neon YouTube channel.

What it is

Shorts Lab is software that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It prepares short educational videos about psychology research for Psychology Neon (@thepsychologyneon), the owner's own channel, and publishes them on a schedule the owner sets.

What it does with YouTube

  • The owner signs in with Google once and connects the Psychology Neon channel.
  • Shorts Lab checks that the connected account is the expected channel, then uploads the channel's own finished videos through the YouTube Data API. Videos are uploaded as private, either to stay private for review or with a scheduled time to become public.
  • It reads back the status of those uploads (processing, privacy and scheduled time) to confirm each one worked. If an upload's confirmation is lost, it looks at the titles of the channel's most recent uploads to find it instead of uploading twice.

It does not change or delete existing videos, and does not read comments, subscribers, analytics or account settings.

Who can use it

Shorts Lab is not a public service and does not accept sign-ups. It is used only by the owner of the Psychology Neon channel to manage that channel.

Privacy

How Google account data is accessed, stored and protected is described in the Shorts Lab privacy policy.
